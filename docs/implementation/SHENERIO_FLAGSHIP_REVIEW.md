# SHENERIO flagship review

**Light. Texture. Ritual.** The redesign opens on a close skincare gesture in warm directional light, carries the same light through the serum-to-cream transition, and then frames texture and ingredient information as a single editorial chapter. Campaign imagery and packaging are private concept studies. The LUMICLEAR product descriptions, £26 serum and £36 cream, usage guidance and ingredient roles come from the supplied SHENERIO material.

## Browser evidence and remaining limits

- **Walkthroughs.** The 28.44-second desktop capture shows the portrait aperture, wheel-driven collection dissolve, product gallery, ingredient index and closing. The 12.28-second phone capture shows the touch menu, section navigation, both product chapters and quick view. [Desktop walkthrough](evidence/flagship/after/flagship-desktop.mp4) · [Phone walkthrough](evidence/flagship/after/flagship-phone.mp4)
- **Build and interaction checks.** Production build and TypeScript passed. All six Chrome scenarios passed at 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 320px, including a separate reduced-motion phone run. The tested states logged zero page or console errors, zero Axe violations and zero measured layout shift. The suite covers wheel and touch input, browser history, keyboard controls, navigation, gallery, ingredient tabs, ritual and footer flows. The focused recording also verifies distinct serum, overlapping-image transfer and cream opacity states, plus mobile menu navigation to Formulation. [Browser QA](evidence/flagship/after/browser-qa.json) · [Motion evidence](evidence/flagship/after/motion-evidence.json)
- **Frame pacing.** The previous and current production builds were measured in cold Chrome contexts over the first 2,640px of the page using the same 2.4-second scripted scroll. The phone-sized context used 4× CPU slowdown, 1.6 Mbps down and 150ms latency. Desktop p95 frame interval moved from **18.3ms to 17.0ms**, with a 16.7ms final median. The throttled phone measured **83.3ms to 83.4ms** at p95 and 16.8ms at the final median, with 11 final intervals over 50ms. Layout shift was zero in the final samples. The gate on compact formulation timelines reduced offscreen work from 127 to 10 style recalculations between the two late-draft samples. Those samples varied, and the gated build still showed long frame intervals under phone throttling. In a separate 480px desktop wheel check, the native comparison jumped directly to its destination in the sampled positions while Lenis produced 29 intermediate positions. This verifies input easing. The lab frame samples and 25fps recordings cannot establish physical-device or field performance. ### Campaign generation prompt

> Create ONE brand-new 3:2 landscape photoreal editorial skincare campaign photograph for SHENERIO, for a 1440px website masthead. Concept LIGHT. TEXTURE. RITUAL. A very close candid beauty portrait in profile/three-quarter: a beautiful adult East Asian woman with warm medium bronze skin, bare skin with fine real pores, minimal grooming and a slick dark bun, eyes lowered, cheek turned toward warm light, elegant fingertips at the lower cheek as if about to apply serum. Frame forehead, nose, full lips and chin with comfortable margin. Her head and hand fill the RIGHT two thirds, the nose faces gently left, her illuminated cheek centered at x=72%, mouth around y=57%; keep enough depth to crop a tall phone portrait from the right half. Dark softly textured black-olive studio everywhere else, especially left 38% deliberately unlit for ivory typography supplied later. Characteristic art direction: a wide soft oblique beam of pale gold window light glances across eye, upper cheek and hand, cutting a diagonal like a folded beam, stronger sculptural dark falloff at lower edges. Cinematic high-end beauty print, natural skin glow, fine analogue grain, rich very dark forest shadows, gold against near black, restrained emotional stillness, tactile and unusual. No flowers, jewellery, extra fingers, bottles, letters, logo, type, bars, borders, graphics, collage, or harsh filter. Not a composite, no reference-brand assets, no text.
