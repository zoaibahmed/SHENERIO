# Phase A / B1 creative plan

User authorised Phase A and B1 on 8 November 2026, with higher ambition and a required evidence-based comparison. Historical audit documents describe the earlier approval boundary; this document records its supersession for the foundation and first homepage slice only.

## Scope

Implement a private, local browser prototype: header and functional collection discovery, campaign hero, House introduction and the two-product LUMICLEAR presentation. Include accessible product preview panels as a small product-discovery interaction study, not full PDPs or transactional commerce. No account, payment, live basket, email collection or remaining homepage sections in B1. Do not populate controls that imply those unbuilt features work.

## Art direction

An immersive, shadow-rich human portrait introduces the brand with a full-width typeset SHENERIO study and an uncluttered headline. An animated image aperture hands off into a light, tactile House composition. Unequal-scale still-life products introduce a deliberate serum/cream pair rather than a repeated grid. Both prices and names stay visible; ingredients are informative, not efficacy proof.

Core colours: Porcelain #FAFAF7, Ink #202723, Mineral #DFE5DF, Slate #58615B, Evergreen #305649, White #FFFFFF. Display: Bodoni Moda; body/UI: Manrope, locally bundled. Verify package font licences and retain them. Wordmark is a typeset concept until official artwork exists.

Layout intent:

```
calm utility header / compact collections disclosure
full-width human campaign / headline left / portrait right
large typographic brand signature
image aperture narrows on scroll into House introduction
House statement left / small cinematic image right / factual prose
LUMICLEAR intro / lead serum stage / inset cream stage
source product information previews / private-preview closing
```

Phone gets a deliberate portrait crop, readable separate text, shorter spatial distances and stacked product stages. No pinning or hover-only access. No repeated slide-up effect on every section.

## Three connected signature moments

1. Hero: controlled portrait settle and masked brand signature, with navigation and primary CTA immediately usable. Brief entrance, no fake loader.
2. Campaign-to-House: native-scroll-linked image aperture and restrained opposing text/media movement. No scroll lock or blank content gap; reduce to static composition on reduced motion.
3. Collection: unequal-stage image masks plus a small hover crop/texture-detail interaction; explicit product preview buttons for touch/keyboard. Product preview uses a real image transition and source ingredient disclosure.

## Pre-build critique

The earlier neutral serif proposal could become generic if every section were a centred statement and a flat product card. Revise it to a shadow-rich opening, physically cropped image handoff, off-centre House composition and unequal product rhythm. Avoid adopting NŪMA's entire layout or decorative category inventory. Generated photography and proposed packaging will be identified as concept assets in the preview and asset register. Do not generate or display sensitive-skin, makeup, performance or certification claims.

## Proof and acceptance

Take actual desktop/phone browser screenshots, inspect them and refine. Exercise menu/disclosure, native anchor navigation, product previews, gallery controls, ingredient accordions, focus return, Escape and reduced motion. Run production build/type checking, accessible-name/contrast scans where available, horizontal-overflow and content checks. Preserve evidence and report failures honestly. No claim of surpassing references without comparison; static evidence alone cannot establish motion/performance superiority.
