NIACINAMIDE: AN ESSENTIAL INGREDIENT IN MODERN SKINCARE.
Niacinamide, also known as vitamin B3, is one of the most widely recognised ingredients 
in contemporary skincare.
Its versatility makes it a popular inclusion in formulations designed to improve the 
appearance of uneven skin tone and support the skin's moisture barrier.
At SHENERIO, niacinamide features in both LUMICLEAR formulations, making it an 
important part of the collection's ingredient story.
