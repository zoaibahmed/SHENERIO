OUR COMMITMENT
At SHENERIO, we believe that understanding the formulation is an important part of the 
skincare experience.
Our approach brings together established cosmetic ingredients, selected for their roles in 
hydration, moisturisation and skin conditioning.
We believe in transparent communication and in helping our customers understand the 
ingredients they choose to incorporate into their daily routines.
Hydration is an important part of maintaining skin that feels comfortable and supple.
Glycerin and sodium hyaluronate are widely used humectant ingredients that help attract 
and retain moisture.
These ingredients contribute to the hydration-focused character of our LUMICLEAR 
formulations.
Panthenol and allantoin are recognised for their skin-conditioning properties.
They contribute to the moisturising and conditioning character of our formulations.
Squalane and shea butter are emollient ingredients used to soften and condition the 
skin.
They contribute to the nourishing qualities of LUMICLEAR RADIANCE CREAM.
Also known as vitamin B3, niacinamide is a multifunctional cosmetic ingredient valued for 
its role in improving the appearance of uneven skin tone and supporting the skin barrier.
It features in both LUMICLEAR formulations.
Selected botanical ingredients, including aloe-derived extract and Centella asiatica 
extract, contribute to the ingredient composition of our moisturiser.
At SHENERIO, formulation is an important part of the experience.
